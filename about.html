Behind Grimiss Ambient

Where the
journey started.

After moving from England to Denmark in 2020, my first tape-machine purchase from a genbrugsbutik — a Danish second-hand shop — was a small shoebox cassette recorder.

I modified it with a speed control and an input control. That simple machine became a way to experiment with sound, and helped reignite my passion for making music with analogue tape.

From that shoebox recorder, the collection grew to include reel-to-reel machines, cassette decks and classic hi-fi equipment, alongside modern synths and digital production. An old Bang & Olufsen reel-to-reel recorder became another part of that journey.

Shaping
with tape.

Grimiss Ambient is built around the meeting point between digital precision and the imperfections of analogue tape.

I create the music digitally, then run it through tape to introduce the warmth, movement and character that only physical recording can bring. Gentle saturation, wow and flutter, noise and the subtle variations of tape all become part of the sound.

The result is a hybrid approach that combines the flexibility of modern production with the character of vintage analogue equipment.

Focus. Relax. Sleep.

FRS means Focus • Relax • Sleep. I make slowly evolving ambient music intended to help you concentrate, unwind or settle down for the night. The sound is there to become part of your surroundings, leaving room for whatever you are doing.

The same recording can accompany reading, studying, creative work, coding and late-night coding, then fit a quieter moment or bedtime. Focus, relaxation and sleep are different ways to use the music; each track can serve more than one purpose.

If you have ADHD and find background sound helpful, you may enjoy using these steady textures while working or as part of a wind-down routine. Everyone listens differently, so choose the sound and volume that feel comfortable for you.

For me, tape gives this music a sense of movement and familiarity: small changes that keep it alive while letting it stay in the background. Explore the collection in the Listening Room.

From listening to creating

The Sound Factory.

The same fascination with tape is also behind The Sound Factory, my upcoming environment for creating soundscapes. Its first instrument, Drone Machine, brings four-track tape-loop recording and sound shaping together.
