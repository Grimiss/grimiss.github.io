Behind Grimiss Ambient

Where the
journey started.

After moving from England to Denmark in 2020, I discovered an old Bang & Olufsen reel-to-reel tape recorder in a local second-hand shop.

That chance find reignited my passion for creating music with analogue tape and became the starting point for Grimiss Ambient.

What began with a single tape machine gradually grew into a studio built around vintage reel-to-reel machines, cassette decks and classic hi-fi equipment, alongside modern synths and digital production.

Shaping
with tape.

Grimiss Ambient is built around the meeting point between digital precision and the imperfections of analogue tape.

I create the music digitally, then run it through tape to introduce the warmth, movement and character that only physical recording can bring. Gentle saturation, wow and flutter, noise and the subtle variations of tape all become part of the sound.

The result is a hybrid approach that combines the flexibility of modern production with the character of vintage analogue equipment.

Focus. Relax. Sleep.

These soundscapes offer space for concentration, a moment to unwind, or company for the quieter hours. You can explore the collection in the Listening Room.

From listening to creating

The Sound Factory.

The same fascination with tape is also behind The Sound Factory, my upcoming environment for creating soundscapes. Its first instrument, Drone Machine, brings four-track tape-loop recording and sound shaping together.
