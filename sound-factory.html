The first instrument

Meet Drone Machine.

A four-track tape-loop instrument at the heart of The Sound Factory. Capture an idea, build layers and let them evolve into something unexpected.

Inspired by physical tape and hands-on studio equipment, it brings recording, sound shaping and performance together in one place.

Small loops.
Endless possibilities.

  • Four tracks to record, layer and explore.
  • Tape character, condition and movement.
  • Tremolo, delay, reverb, phaser and stereo width.
  • Visual scopes that respond to your sound.

The Sound Factory is the environment. Drone Machine is its first instrument, with more machines planned.
