FRS means Focus • Relax • Sleep. The same track can accompany study, creative work, coding, a quiet evening or bedtime. Choose by the sound and atmosphere you enjoy; there is no prescribed way to listen.

If you have ADHD and find background sound helpful, these slowly evolving textures can be a companion for focused tasks or winding down. Try a soundscape during coding or late-night coding, when you want a steady backdrop without lyrics competing for your attention.

Start at a comfortable, low volume and let the music sit behind what you are doing. For rest, choose a longer recording if you prefer fewer interruptions. Change tracks whenever the sound no longer suits your mood.
