The process

From digital
to soundscape.

The music begins in the computer, then takes a journey through physical tape. Digital composition, analogue character and a little unpredictability along the way.

01 / Compose

Digital.

Each piece starts in Reaper. Synths, samples, effects and layered textures form the foundation of a slowly evolving composition.

Small changes in tone, movement and space let the sound develop gradually, with room for an idea to find its own direction.

02 / Transform

Tape.

I send the material out of the computer and through real analogue tape machines. Saturation, compression, hiss and variations in speed add movement and character to the recording.

Cassette decks, portable recorders and reel-to-reel machines each bring something different. Sometimes tape adds a subtle finishing touch; sometimes its imperfections become the centre of the piece.

03 / Shape

Soundscape.

The tape recordings return to the computer, where I layer, extend and refine them into the finished long-form soundscape.

The same music can accompany focus, relaxation or sleep — studying, coding late into the night, unwinding or settling down for bed. These are different ways to use one recording, rather than separate categories.

Finally, the sound is paired with footage of the physical machines and tapes that help shape the identity of Grimiss Ambient.
